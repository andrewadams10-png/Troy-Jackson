After Platner, Troy Jackson Carries On Crusade to Unseat Collins

PORTLAND, Maine, Oct. 2026. Aroostock County native, fourth generation logger and former Maine Senate president Troy Jackson is running as a progressive Democrat, seeking to unseat Sen. Susan Collins (R-ME). See where he stands on the issues that matter most to Mainers.

Healthcare

Troy Jackson supports universal healthcare and promises to support the Medicare for All Act if elected to the Senate. Growing up near the Canadian border, Jackson says he recalls neighbors driving to Canada to purchase medicines that were too expensive in the United States. He supports the federal government's ability to negotiate for lower drug prices.

Susan Collins believes that the healthcare system requires reform to lower drug prices but opposes Medicare for All.

Economy

Troy Jackson supports raising income taxes on high earners and promises to roll back current tax breaks for the wealthiest Americans. He has not given a figure for the tax increase, saying, "I'm sure we'll figure out what that percentage is going to be when I get there."

Foreign Policy

Troy Jackson opposes military aid to Israel and has referred to Israel's war in Gaza as a genocide. He has referred to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu as a war criminal

Susan Collins supports a continued alliance between the U.S. and Israel

Congressional Reform

Troy Jackson supports a ban on members of Congress trading stocks and supports a plan to force sitting members of Congress to divest from assets.

Susan Collins has previously opposed similar bans.
